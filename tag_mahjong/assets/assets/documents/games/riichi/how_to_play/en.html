How to Play Riichi Mahjong

Japanese Mahjong with yaku, riichi and dora.

The Goal

Complete a hand of four sets and a pair (or seven pairs, or the Thirteen Orphans) that contains at least one yaku (scoring pattern). Everyone starts with 25,000 points; the highest score after the chosen number of hands wins.

Setup

136 tiles (no flowers). The wall shows a dora indicator: the next tile in sequence is a dora and adds one han to a winning hand for each copy you hold.

Playing

  • On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Tap a tile once to lift it and tap it again to discard.
  • When another player discards, you may claim the tile: Chow (a run of three in one suit, only from the player before you), Pung (three identical tiles) or Kong (four identical tiles, which also gives you a replacement tile). A win beats a pung/kong, and a pung/kong beats a chow.
  • You can also declare a concealed kong from four identical tiles in your hand, or add a fourth tile to one of your pungs.
  • Riichi: with a fully concealed hand that is one tile from winning, tap Riichi and discard one of the highlighted tiles. You pay 1,000 points into the pot and cannot change your hand afterwards, but Riichi is a yaku and the pot goes to the winner.

Yaku Included

Riichi, Menzen Tsumo, Tanyao, Yakuhai (dragons, seat wind, prevailing wind), Pinfu, Iipeiko, Chiitoitsu, Toitoi, Sanshoku Doujun, Ittsu, Honitsu, Chinitsu, Sanankou, Honroutou, Shousangen, Haitei/Houtei, Rinshan Kaihou and dora. Yakuman: Kokushi Musou, Daisangen, Suuankou and Tsuuiisou.

Scoring

Han decide the payment (mangan at 5 han, haneman at 6, baiman at 8, sanbaiman at 11, yakuman at 13). This edition uses a fixed 30 fu (25 for seven pairs). A hand may not win on a tile it has previously discarded (furiten).
