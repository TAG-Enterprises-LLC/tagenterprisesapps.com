How to Play Chinese Official Mahjong

Tournament Mahjong: you need 8 points to win.

The Goal

Win with a hand worth at least 8 points, not counting flowers. Points come from the fan patterns in your hand; every player pays the winner.

Setup

144 tiles with flowers. Each player gets 13 tiles; the dealer starts with 14.

Playing

  • On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Tap a tile once to lift it and tap it again to discard.
  • When another player discards, you may claim the tile: Chow (a run of three in one suit, only from the player before you), Pung (three identical tiles) or Kong (four identical tiles, which also gives you a replacement tile). A win beats a pung/kong, and a pung/kong beats a chow.
  • You can also declare a concealed kong from four identical tiles in your hand, or add a fourth tile to one of your pungs.
  • Flowers and seasons are bonus tiles: they are set aside automatically and replaced from the end of the wall.

Patterns Included

Big Four Winds (88), Big Three Dragons (88), Thirteen Orphans (88), Little Four Winds (64), Little Three Dragons (64), All Honors (64), All Terminals (64), Four Concealed Pungs (64), Full Flush (24), Seven Pairs (24), Pure Straight (16), Mixed Triple Chow (8), Last Tile Draw (8), Out with Replacement Tile (8), Half Flush (6), All Pungs (6), Fully Concealed Hand (4), dragon/seat/prevailing-wind pungs (2), Concealed Hand (2), All Chows (2), All Simples (2), Self-Drawn (1) and flowers (1 each, bonus only).

Payment

A self-drawn win is paid 8 + the hand's points by every opponent. A discard win is paid 8 + the points by the discarder and 8 by each other player.
