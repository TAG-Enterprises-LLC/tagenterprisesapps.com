How to Play Sichuan Mahjong

No chows, no honors, and one suit must go.

The Goal

Complete four sets (pungs or kongs) and a pair, or seven pairs. Sichuan uses only the 108 suit tiles: no honors, no flowers and no chows.

The Void Suit

Before the first discard, each player chooses one suit to give up. You must discard every tile of that suit before any other, you cannot claim it, and you cannot win while you still hold one.

Playing

  • Draw a tile and discard one. Tap a tile once to lift it and again to discard.
  • Claim another player's discard as a Pung or Kong, or declare Mahjong.
  • You may declare concealed kongs and add to your pungs.

Scoring

Every win is worth at least 1 point, and each fan doubles the payment. Patterns that add fan: Pure One Suit (+2), All Pungs (+1), Seven Pairs (+2), Self-Drawn (+1), each Kong (+1), each set of four identical tiles in hand (+1), Last Tile (+1) and Win on Kong (+1). Payment is 2 to the power of the fan (capped at 8 fan): a discard win is paid by the discarder, a self-drawn win by all three opponents. This edition ends the hand at the first win.
