How to Play Mahjong Solitaire

Clear the board by matching free tiles.

The Goal

Remove every tile by matching pairs. Every deal can be cleared, but the order you play matters.

Free Tiles

A tile is free when nothing lies on top of it and it can slide out to the left or the right. Blocked tiles are dimmed.

Matching

Tap a free tile, then tap a second free tile that matches it. Identical tiles match; any flower matches any other flower, and any season matches any other season.

Tools

  • Hint highlights a free matching pair.
  • Undo takes back your last pair.
  • Shuffle re-deals the tiles that are left when you get stuck (it cannot fix a stack that has no way out; then undo or start a new game).
  • Pinch to zoom in on the big Turtle layout.

Layouts

Turtle (144 tiles), Pyramid (62 tiles) and Fortress (68 tiles). Your best times are saved in Games History.
