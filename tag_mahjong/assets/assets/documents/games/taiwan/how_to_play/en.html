How to Play Taiwanese Mahjong

16-tile Mahjong scored in tai.

The Goal

Complete five sets and a pair from 17 tiles, worth at least the minimum tai chosen in Settings (default 1).

Setup

144 tiles with flowers. Each player is dealt 16 tiles; the dealer starts with 17. There are no seven-pairs or Thirteen Orphans hands.

Playing

  • On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Tap a tile once to lift it and tap it again to discard.
  • When another player discards, you may claim the tile: Chow (a run of three in one suit, only from the player before you), Pung (three identical tiles) or Kong (four identical tiles, which also gives you a replacement tile). A win beats a pung/kong, and a pung/kong beats a chow.
  • You can also declare a concealed kong from four identical tiles in your hand, or add a fourth tile to one of your pungs.
  • Flowers and seasons are bonus tiles: they are set aside automatically and replaced from the end of the wall.

Scoring

Tai come from Dealer (1, plus 2 per consecutive dealer win), Concealed Self-Draw (3), Self-Drawn (1), Concealed Hand (1), dragon/seat/prevailing-wind pungs (1 each), All Chows (2), All Pungs (4), Mixed One Suit (4), Pure One Suit (8), three to five concealed pungs (2 / 5 / 8), Small/Great Three Dragons (4/8), Small/Great Four Winds (8/16), All Honors (16), matching flowers (1 each), four flowers or seasons (2 each), all eight bonus tiles (8), Last Tile (1) and Win on Kong (1).

A hand pays 30 + 10 per tai: the discarder pays for a discard win; all three opponents pay for a self-drawn win.
