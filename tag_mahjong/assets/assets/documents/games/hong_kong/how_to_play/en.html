How to Play Hong Kong Mahjong

Fast, classic Mahjong scored in faan.

The Goal

Be the first to complete a hand of four sets (chows, pungs or kongs) and a pair, worth at least the minimum faan chosen in Settings (default 3). Play the number of hands set in Settings; the highest score at the end wins.

Setup

144 tiles: three suits (dots, bamboo, characters), winds, dragons and 8 bonus tiles. Each player gets 13 tiles; the dealer (East) starts with 14. The dealer stays as dealer after winning or a drawn hand; otherwise the deal passes on.

Playing

  • On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Tap a tile once to lift it and tap it again to discard.
  • When another player discards, you may claim the tile: Chow (a run of three in one suit, only from the player before you), Pung (three identical tiles) or Kong (four identical tiles, which also gives you a replacement tile). A win beats a pung/kong, and a pung/kong beats a chow.
  • You can also declare a concealed kong from four identical tiles in your hand, or add a fourth tile to one of your pungs.
  • Flowers and seasons are bonus tiles: they are set aside automatically and replaced from the end of the wall.

Winning and Scoring

Declare Mahjong on a self-drawn tile or on another player's discard. Faan come from patterns such as Self-Drawn (1), Concealed Hand (1), dragon, seat-wind and prevailing-wind pungs (1 each), Common Hand (1), All Pungs (3), Mixed One Suit (3), Pure One Suit (7), Small/Great Three Dragons (5/8), Small/Great Four Winds (6/13), All Honors (10), Thirteen Orphans (13) and matching flowers (1 each). The limit is 13 faan.

A discard win is paid by the discarder; a self-drawn win is paid by all three opponents.

Tips

  • Collect dragon and wind pungs early – they add faan quickly.
  • Stay concealed when you can: a concealed self-drawn win is worth 3 faan.
  • Use the Hint button for a suggested discard.
