How to Play Hearts

Avoid the hearts and the Queen of Spades.

The Goal

Finish with the lowest score. Every heart you take costs 1 point and the Queen of Spades costs 13. The game ends when a player reaches the target chosen in Settings; the lowest score wins.

Dealing and Passing

Each player gets 13 cards. Before playing, pick three cards to pass: left, right, across, then no passing, repeating each round (you can turn passing off in Settings). Cards you receive are outlined in blue.

Playing

  • The player with the 2 of clubs leads it to start the round.
  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you cannot, play any card – but no hearts or Queen of Spades on the very first trick if you have other cards.
  • The highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads the next one.
  • Hearts cannot be led until a heart has been played ("hearts broken"), unless you hold only hearts.

Scoring

After 13 tricks, each player adds the points in the tricks they won: 1 per heart and 13 for the Queen of Spades, 26 in all.

Shoot the moon: if one player wins every heart and the Queen of Spades, that player scores 0 and everyone else scores 26.

Tips

  • Pass your highest cards, especially the Queen of Spades and high spades.
  • Slip just under the current winner with your highest safe card.
  • Use the Hint button for a suggested card.
