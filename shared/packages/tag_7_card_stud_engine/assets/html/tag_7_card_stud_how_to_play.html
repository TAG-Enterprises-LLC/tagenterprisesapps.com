T-G-Apps 7 Card Stud — How to Play

Rules and gameplay guide for T-G-Apps 7 Card Stud poker.

T-G-Apps 7 Card Stud is a fixed-limit poker game for two to six human or robot players. There are no community cards. Each player may receive as many as seven cards and uses the best five-card poker hand at showdown.

1. Setup

  • Choose two to six players.
  • Select Create Local Game, then choose two to six players, enter each name, and mark each player as Human or Robot. Player 1 is always human.
  • Enter player names and open Settings to choose the starting bank, ante, bring-in, small bet, and big bet.
  • Select Reset to Default Settings to restore the T-G-Apps defaults.

2. Ante and Initial Deal

Each active player posts the ante. Every player then receives two private cards face down and one public card face up. This opening round is called Third Street.

3. Bring-In and Third Street

The player showing the lowest face-up card posts the bring-in. Play continues around the table. Players may fold, call the current amount, or complete and raise according to the fixed-limit rules.

4. Fourth Street

Each remaining player receives a second face-up card. Betting uses the small-bet limit. The exposed hand selected by the game acts first.

5. Fifth and Sixth Streets

Each remaining player receives one additional face-up card on Fifth Street and again on Sixth Street. Betting uses the big-bet limit on both streets.

6. Seventh Street

Each remaining player receives one final private card face down. The last betting round uses the big-bet limit.

7. Betting Actions

  • Check: Stay in the hand without adding chips when no bet is outstanding.
  • Call: Match the current bet.
  • Bet: Open the betting when no bet is outstanding.
  • Raise: Increase the current bet by the street’s fixed limit.
  • Fold: Give up the hand and any chips already committed to the pot.

Each betting round permits up to the configured maximum number of raises. A player can act only when it is that player’s turn.

8. Showdown

After the final betting round, the remaining players reveal their private cards. The best five-card poker hand wins the pot. Equal winning hands split the pot.

9. Hand Rankings

  1. Royal Flush
  2. Straight Flush
  3. Four of a Kind
  4. Full House
  5. Flush
  6. Straight
  7. Three of a Kind
  8. Two Pair
  9. One Pair
  10. High Card

10. Robot Players

Robot players act automatically using the information available to them. Robots do not know unrevealed cards or upcoming cards. Player 1 always remains human.

11. Multi-Phone Play

  1. On the host phone, select Multi-Phone Play and Create Multi-Phone Game. Choose two to six players and enter every player name.
  2. Player 1 is the host. Other players may be Humans on the host phone, Humans on remote phones, or Robots in the host game. At least one Remote Human is required.
  3. Share the six-character room code. Each remote player selects Join Game, enters the code, and claims their name from the host-defined list.
  4. A remote name can be claimed only once. If someone claims the wrong name, the host must cancel that room and create a new one.
  5. The host selects Start Game after every Remote Human name has been claimed.

Remote players are always human and may not be robots. Additional humans may share the host phone, and all robots remain part of the host game. Private cards are shown only on the phone that controls the seat. Public cards, bets, turns, and results synchronize across all participating devices.

12. Starting a New Hand

After the current hand is complete, select New Hand to continue. In a multi-phone room, only the host starts the next hand.

13. Entertainment Only

T-G-Apps 7 Card Stud does not offer real-money gambling, prizes, wagers, or cash-out functionality.
