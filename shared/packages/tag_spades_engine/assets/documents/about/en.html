About T-G-Apps Spades

The classic partnership trick-taking card game.

T-G-Apps Spades brings the classic partnership card game to your phone. Play against robot opponents, or with friends on one phone, at your own pace.

Features

  • Two partnerships of two: you and the seat across from you share a bid and a score
  • Bid a number of tricks, or bid Nil for a bonus if you take none at all
  • Spades are always trump: the highest spade wins the trick
  • Hints, game history and adjustable game length
