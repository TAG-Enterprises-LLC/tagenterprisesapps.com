About T-G-Apps 5 Card Draw

Five-card draw-poker entertainment by T-G-Apps LLC.

T-G-Apps 5 Card Draw is a phone and web poker simulation developed by T-G-Apps LLC. It supports two to six players, human and robot opponents, fixed-limit betting, a single three-card draw, split pots, saved settings, player history, hints, and shared T-G-Apps presentation.
