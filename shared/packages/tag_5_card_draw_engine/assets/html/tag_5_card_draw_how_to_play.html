T-G-Apps 5 Card Draw — How to Play

Rules and gameplay guide for five-card draw poker.

T-G-Apps 5 Card Draw uses a standard 52-card deck with no jokers. Two to six human or robot players compete for the pot.

1. Setup

  • Choose two to six players.
  • Enter names and select human or robot players.
  • Open Settings to choose the starting bank, ante, and fixed Bet/Raise limit.
  • The last settings used are saved. Select Reset to Default Settings to restore the T-G-Apps defaults.

2. Ante and Deal

Each active player pays the ante, then receives five cards. A player who cannot pay the ante is removed from later hands.

3. Opening Betting

Players may Check or Call, Bet or Raise, or Fold. Betting is fixed-limit: every Bet or Raise uses the amount selected in Settings. Each betting round permits up to three raises. A player who cannot fully call must fold; this release does not use side pots.

4. Draw

Each remaining human player may select and replace zero to three cards. Select Stand Pat to keep all five. Robot players choose their discards automatically.

5. Final Betting

After the draw, remaining players complete a second fixed-limit betting round using the same Check/Call, Bet/Raise, and Fold actions.

6. Showdown

Remaining hands are revealed. The best five-card poker hand wins the pot. Equal winning hands split the pot, with any indivisible remainder distributed by the game.

7. Hand Rankings

  1. Royal Flush
  2. Straight Flush
  3. Four of a Kind
  4. Full House
  5. Flush
  6. Straight
  7. Three of a Kind
  8. Two Pair
  9. One Pair
  10. High Card

8. History

Custom-named human players may continue with their saved bank or start over. Up to 500 named players are retained. Default names such as Player 1 are not stored.

9. Entertainment Only

T-G-Apps 5 Card Draw does not offer real-money gambling, prizes, wagers, or cash-out functionality.
