TAG Holdem Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026

TAG Holdem is provided by TAG Enterprises LLC. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Do Not Collect

TAG Holdem does not require account registration and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, street address, or payment information.

Local Game Information

The app may store game-related information on your device, such as player names entered by you, game history, settings, music preferences, and saved bank amounts. This information is used only to support gameplay and app features on your device.

Advertising

The app may display ads through third-party advertising services. Those services may use device or usage information according to their own privacy policies. TAG Enterprises LLC does not directly control third-party advertising data practices.

Children's Privacy

TAG Holdem is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

TAG Enterprises LLC does not sell personal information. Game information stored locally by the app is not intentionally transmitted to TAG Enterprises LLC.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be included with the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact TAG Enterprises LLC through the support information provided with the app or website.
