About T-G-Apps Farkle

A push-your-luck dice game by T-G-Apps.

T-G-Apps Farkle is a phone and web dice game for 1 to 6 players on one device, human or robot, with hints, background music and nine languages.

Players can also share one game from several phones and the web, joining with a room code.

Robot Players

The robots play to win, not just to score points. With two players, the robot follows an exact mathematical solution of the game. With three to six players, it uses a strategy tuned over millions of simulated games.

The robot does not cheat; it does not know what the dice will show.

Hints show what the robot would do. Because it plays to win, a hint can look surprising, such as banking a small total late in the game or rolling on with few dice when far behind.
