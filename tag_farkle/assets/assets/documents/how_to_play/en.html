T-G-Apps Farkle — How to Play

Rules and scoring for T-G-Apps Farkle.

Objective

Be the first to reach the target score: 5,000, 10,000 or 20,000 points. When a player reaches it, every other player gets one last turn, and the highest score wins.

Your Turn

  • Roll all six dice.
  • Tap scoring dice to set them aside; they turn green. After every roll you must set aside at least one scoring die or combination.
  • Bank to add the turn total to your score and end your turn, or roll the remaining dice to try for more.
  • Farkle: if a roll has no scoring dice, you lose the points collected this turn and your turn ends.
  • Hot dice: if all six dice have scored, roll all six again and keep adding to the same turn total.
  • Combinations only count within a single roll.

Getting on the Board

Before your first bank, you need at least 500 points in a single turn.

Scoring

  • Single 1: 100
  • Single 5: 50
  • Three 1s: 1,000
  • Three of a kind (2–6): 100 × the number (three 4s = 400)
  • Four of a kind: 1,000
  • Five of a kind: 2,000
  • Six of a kind: 3,000
  • Straight 1–6: 1,500
  • Three pairs: 1,500
  • Four of a kind plus a pair: 1,500
  • Two triplets: 2,500

When dice can be counted in more than one way, you always get the best total.

Options

  • Target Score: 5,000, 10,000 or 20,000.
  • Three-Farkle Penalty: three Farkles in a row cost 1,000 points. Scores can go below zero.
  • Robot players and robot speed; background music and sound effects.

Playing on Several Phones

  • On the host phone, set each player who uses another phone to Remote, then tap Start Game. The app shows a six-character Room code.
  • On each other phone, tap Join Game and enter the code and your name, then mark yourself ready. The host starts the game when everyone is ready.
  • Players on the host phone (Human) pass it between turns, and the host phone plays the robots. Everyone sees every roll as it happens.
  • Each player on another phone chooses in the lobby what happens if their phone disconnects: their turns are skipped, or the robot plays for them until the phone reconnects. If the host leaves, the game ends for everyone.

Hints

While choosing dice, tap Hint to see what the expert robot would do, its chance of winning, the next best choice, and the chance of a Farkle on the next roll.

When the game situation changes the best play, the hint also says why: for example, a player is close to winning, you are protecting a lead, or it is your last turn. In a one-player game, hints show the average points for the turn instead of a chance of winning.
