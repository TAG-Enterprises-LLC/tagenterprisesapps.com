Common Blackjack Survey Abbreviations

Blackjack survey sites often use short rule codes. The exact meaning can vary by publisher, so always check the source page, but these are common meanings used in the Wizard of Vegas survey.

Code Meaning
h17Dealer hits soft 17.
s17Dealer stands on soft 17.
dsDouble down is allowed after splitting.
d10Double down is restricted to two-card totals of 10 or more.
lsLate surrender is available.
rsaResplitting aces is allowed.
nmNo mid-game entry.
nmmNo mid-game entry and no mid-game spreading to multiple hands.
nmsNo mid-game entry and no sitting out hands; dropping from two hands to one may prevent returning to two hands until the shuffle.
scA card is used to set the shuffle point in single-deck or double-deck games; often called a cut card.
pvPenetration varies by dealer.
notchPenetration is set by a notch.
shoeSingle-deck or double-deck cards are dealt from a shoe.

Abbreviation guide included for convenience. Visit the original survey site for the publisher's current definitions and usage notes.
