TAG 21 - HOW TO PLAY

TAG 21 is a blackjack-style casino card game. The goal is to beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 than the dealer without going over 21.

OBJECT OF THE GAME

Each player tries to make a hand total as close to 21 as possible. A hand that goes over 21 is busted and loses. After all players finish their hands, the dealer plays. Hands that beat the dealer win. Hands that tie the dealer push. Hands lower than the dealer lose.

CARD VALUES

Number cards count as their printed value.

Jacks, Queens, and Kings count as 10.

Aces count as either 1 or 11, whichever gives the best legal hand total without busting.

A blackjack is a two-card 21 made from an Ace and a 10-value card.

DECK SHOE

At setup, Player 1 chooses the shoe size. The game supports a 6-deck or 8-deck shoe. The selected shoe is used for the hand.

CUT CARD AND BURN CARD

The shoe includes a cut-card style flow. When the shoe is prepared, the game manages cut and burn behavior internally. Burn cards are not part of player or dealer hands and are not available for betting decisions.

PLAYER SETUP

The game supports multiple players. Players may be human or robot players.

During setup, each human player may enter a custom player name.

Default names such as "Player 1", "Player 2", etc. are treated as temporary names and are not stored in player history.

PLAYER HISTORY SYSTEM

TAG 21 includes a persistent player history system for named human players.

The game stores:

* Player name
* Most recent ending Bank amount
* Last date and time the player was used

The game stores up to 500 player records.

When a player enters a custom name during setup, the game checks the stored history.

If the name already exists, the game displays the stored Bank amount and offers these choices:

CONTINUE PREVIOUS BANK

Continue using the saved Bank amount from the player's previous session.

START OVER

Reset the player's Bank back to the standard starting Bank of $1,000.00. The stored history entry is updated with the new Bank amount.

CHANGE NAME

Return to name entry so a different player name may be used.

At the end of each game session, the player's current Bank amount is automatically saved into history.

If the player name is new and the history list is already full, the game replaces the oldest unused player record.

BANKROLL

Each player begins with a $1,000.00 bank unless a saved Bank is restored from player history.

The bank area shows the player's remaining chips and Bank Total.

BETTING

Before cards are dealt, each active player places a bet.

Minimum bet: $10.00.

A player cannot bet more than the value remaining in that player's bank.

The betting area includes a horizontal scrolling chip selection bar.

Tap a chip denomination to add that amount to the current bet.

The betting message area displays prompts such as:

* Place bet; press Next Player
* Place bet; press Deal Cards
* Next Player
* Dealer Play

REPEAT BET

The Repeat Bet button automatically restores the player's previous wager amount.

Repeat Bet is disabled if the player does not have enough Bank remaining to repeat the prior wager.

UNDO

Undo removes the most recently added chip from the current wager.

ALL IN

The All In button moves the player's entire remaining bank into the betting area. This commits all available chips as the current bet.

PLAY HAND

Play Hand locks the current bet and advances the game.

The game will not continue if the bet is below the $10.00 minimum.

The game will not allow a bet greater than the player's available bank.

AUTOMATIC CHANGE MAKING

If a player taps a denomination that is not currently available, the game attempts to make change.

If the player has a larger chip, the game can break that larger chip into smaller chips and complete the selected bet.

Example: If the player taps a $5 chip but has no $5 chips, and has a $10 chip, the game can change the $10 chip into two $5 chips, move one $5 chip to the bet, and leave the other $5 chip in the bank.

If the player has enough value in smaller chips, the game can place the selected bet using those smaller chips.

If the bank does not contain enough value to cover the requested bet, no chips are moved and the game displays an explanatory message.

INITIAL DEAL

After all required bets are placed, the dealer deals two cards to each player and two cards to the dealer.

The dealer's down card is shown as a card back until the dealer reveals it. The value of the down card is determined when dealt, but it is hidden from the player until the correct point in the hand.

INSURANCE

If the dealer's visible up card is an Ace, insurance may be offered.

Insurance is a side bet that the dealer has blackjack. The insurance bet is separate from the player's main bet.

If insurance is taken, the game displays:

"Insurance Taken, press Continue"

If the dealer has blackjack, insurance pays according to blackjack insurance rules and the main hand is then resolved.

If the dealer does not have blackjack, the insurance bet is lost and the hand continues normally.

PLAYER ACTIONS

HIT

Hit deals one additional card to the active hand.

If the hand total goes over 21, the hand busts and loses.

STAY

Stay keeps the current hand total and ends action for that hand.

DOUBLE DOWN

Double Down doubles the active hand bet, deals exactly one more card, and ends action for that hand.

Double Down is available only when the player has enough bank to add the extra bet.

SPLIT

If the first two cards in a hand have the same blackjack value, the player may split them into two separate hands.

Splitting requires an additional bet equal to the original hand bet.

Each split hand receives another card and is played separately.

If a new split hand again has a pair, it may be split again if the game allows it and the player has enough bank.

When moving between split hands, the game displays:

* Player X Next Hand
* Next Player
* Dealer Play

depending on remaining hands and players.

ROBOT PLAYERS

Robot players make betting and playing decisions automatically.

During betting, robot players automatically place their bets.

During hand play, the Robot Play button advances the robot one action at a time so human players can watch the robot:

* Hit
* Stay
* Bust
* Split
* Double Down

The robot's cards and totals remain visible during play.

After the robot finishes its hand, the button changes to Next Player or Dealer Play.

DEALER PLAY

After all player hands are complete, the dealer reveals the down card.

The dealer hits until reaching 17 or better.

If the dealer busts, all non-busted player hands win.

HAND RESULTS

A winning hand returns the player's bet and pays equal winnings.

A losing hand loses the bet.

A push returns the player's bet with no winnings.

A busted hand loses immediately.

If the dealer has blackjack at the start, the hand ends immediately for standard non-blackjack hands.

ODDS AND POINT COUNTS

During human play, the game displays the current point count for the dealer and each player in order.

The game may also display the estimated odds of winning the current player hand based on visible cards and the current game state. When the player hits and receives another card, the odds are recalculated.

NEW HAND

After a hand ends, New Hand starts another hand using the continuing player banks.

If a player's bank is exhausted, that player cannot continue betting until the game provides a reset or new-game flow.

END GAME / EXIT

At the end of a game, each named human player's ending Bank amount is automatically stored in the player history database.

If the TAG exit flow is enabled, an interstitial ad may be shown before the exit splash images are displayed and the app returns to the phone menu.

SETTINGS

Settings may include background music controls, volume controls, and other game options.
