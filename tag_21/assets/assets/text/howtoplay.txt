TAG 21 - HOW TO PLAY

WELCOME TO TAG 21

TAG 21 is a blackjack-style casino card game developed by TAG Enterprises LLC.

The objective is simple:

Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 than the dealer without going over 21.

TAG 21 supports:
• Human and robot players
• Multi-player gameplay
• Persistent player history
• Multiple deck shoes
• Splits, doubles, insurance, and blackjack payouts
• Dynamic odds displays
• Background music and customizable visuals

---

## OBJECT OF THE GAME

Each player attempts to build a hand total closer to 21 than the dealer.

A hand that exceeds 21 busts and immediately loses.

After all players finish their hands, the dealer plays.

Results:
• Higher than dealer without busting = WIN
• Same as dealer = PUSH
• Lower than dealer = LOSS
• Bust = LOSS

---

## CARD VALUES

• Number cards count as their printed value.
• Jacks, Queens, and Kings count as 10.
• Aces count as either 1 or 11, whichever produces the best legal total.

BLACKJACK

A blackjack is a two-card 21 made with:
• An Ace
• Any 10-value card

Blackjack pays 3:2.

---

## DECK SHOE

At setup, Player 1 selects:
• 6-deck shoe
or
• 8-deck shoe

The selected shoe remains active until reshuffled.

---

## CUT CARD / SHUFFLE FLOW

TAG 21 uses casino-style shoe management.

A virtual cut card is placed near the back portion of the shoe.

When the cut-card point is reached:
• the current hand finishes normally
• the shoe is reshuffled
• a new cut-card position is created

Burn-card behavior is handled internally.

---

## PLAYER SETUP

TAG 21 supports:
• 1 to 6 players
• Any combination of human and robot players

Human players may enter custom names.

Default names such as:
• Player 1
• Player 2

are temporary and are not saved into history.

---

## PLAYER HISTORY SYSTEM

TAG 21 includes a persistent player-history database for named human players.

The game stores:
• Player name
• Most recent ending Bank amount
• Last date/time used

Up to 500 player records are retained.

When a returning player enters a saved name, the game offers:

CONTINUE PREVIOUS BANK
Continue using the saved Bank amount.

START OVER
Reset Bank to the standard $1,000.00 starting Bank.

CHANGE NAME
Return to name entry.

At game completion, named human players automatically save their latest Bank totals.

---

## BANKROLL

Each player normally begins with:
$1,000.00

unless restored from saved player history.

The Bank area displays:
• Current Bank amount
• Chip inventory
• Active wager

---

## BETTING

Before cards are dealt, each active player places a wager.

Minimum bet:
$10.00

The game will not allow:
• bets below minimum
• bets larger than available Bank

The betting interface includes:
• scrolling chip bar
• repeat bet
• undo
• all in
• automatic change making

---

## REPEAT BET

Repeat Bet restores the previous wager automatically.

Repeat Bet is disabled if the player lacks sufficient funds.

---

## UNDO

Undo removes the most recently added betting chip.

---

## ALL IN

All In moves the player's remaining Bank into the active wager.

---

## AUTOMATIC CHANGE MAKING

TAG 21 automatically makes change when possible.

Example:
If the player taps a chip denomination unavailable in the Bank, the game may break a larger chip into smaller chips automatically.

If insufficient funds exist, the game displays an explanatory message.

---

## INITIAL DEAL

After all required bets are complete:
• each player receives two cards
• the dealer receives two cards

One dealer card remains hidden until dealer play begins.

---

## INSURANCE

If the dealer shows an Ace, insurance may be offered.

Insurance is a side wager that the dealer has blackjack.

If insurance is taken:
• the game displays a confirmation message
• dealer blackjack is checked internally

If dealer has blackjack:
• insurance pays according to standard blackjack rules

If dealer does not have blackjack:
• insurance wager is lost
• normal play continues

---

## PLAYER ACTIONS

HIT

Adds one additional card.

Hands exceeding 21 bust immediately.

---

STAY

Ends action for the current hand.

---

DOUBLE DOWN

Double Down:
• doubles the wager
• deals exactly one additional card
• automatically ends the hand

Double Down requires sufficient remaining Bank.

---

SPLIT

If the first two cards have the same blackjack value, the hand may be split.

Splitting:
• creates two separate hands
• requires an additional matching wager
• deals additional cards to each split hand

Additional splits may be allowed if:
• the hand qualifies
• sufficient Bank remains

Split-hand transitions may display:
• Player X Next Hand
• Next Player
• Dealer Play

---

## ROBOT PLAYERS

Robot players automatically:
• place wagers
• make gameplay decisions

Robot gameplay is intentionally slowed so players can observe decisions.

Robot actions may include:
• Hit
• Stay
• Double Down
• Split
• Bust

Robot cards remain visible during play.

---

## ODDS DISPLAY

TAG 21 may display:
• current hand totals
• estimated winning percentages
• dynamically updated odds after hits

Odds calculations use:
• visible cards
• remaining shoe state
• current dealer/player conditions

---

## DEALER PLAY

After all player hands finish:
• dealer reveals the hidden card
• dealer draws until reaching required total

If dealer busts:
all remaining non-busted player hands win.

---

## HAND RESULTS

WIN
Player receives wager plus winnings.

LOSS
Player loses wager.

PUSH
Original wager returned.

BLACKJACK
Pays 3:2.

BUST
Immediate loss.

---

## BROKE PLAYER REMOVAL

If a player Bank falls below the minimum $10.00 wager requirement:

• the player is removed from the current game
• a broke-player notification screen appears
• the player may participate again in a future game with a reset Bank

After acknowledgment:
• remaining players continue
or
• the game exits if no players remain active

---

## NEW HAND

New Hand begins another round using current remaining player Banks.

Players with insufficient funds are removed automatically.

---

## END GAME / EXIT

At game completion:
• named human players automatically save their latest Bank totals into player history

Depending on configuration:
• an interstitial ad may appear during exit flow
• TAG exit splash graphics may display before returning to the phone menu
