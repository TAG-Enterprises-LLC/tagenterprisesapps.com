App 01

T&G YZ

A five-dice scorecard strategy game for one to six players. Roll up to three times per turn, hold the dice you want to keep, and choose the best open scoring category. The expanded scorecard includes Ones through Sixes, Small and Large Straights, Full House, Two Pair, Three-, Four-, and Five-of-a-Kind, a Five-of-a-Kind bonus, and Oops as a flexible fallback.

Human and robot players can share one device, while private multi-phone games synchronize dice holds, rolls, scoring, turns, and final results. The strategy-based robot and Hint system evaluate open categories and fallback choices without knowing future rolls.

  • 1–6 players
  • Human and robot play
  • Private multi-phone rooms
  • Saved game history
  • Upper-section and Five-of-a-Kind bonuses
  • Music and celebrations

App 02

T&G 21

A configurable blackjack simulation for one to six players. Place chip-based bets, receive hints based on the selected table rules, and choose Hit, Stand, Double, Split, Surrender, Insurance, or Even Money when legal. Player banks continue between games, named human results are recorded, and the shoe automatically reshuffles after the yellow cut card is reached.

Standard play supports human and robot opponents. Private multi-phone rooms are human-only, keep the dealer hole card and shoe protected by the server, and synchronize bets, hands, dealer play, results, and bankrolls across participating phones.

  • 1–6 players
  • Detailed casino rule settings
  • Chip betting and Repeat Bet
  • Hints and robot strategy
  • Private multi-phone rooms
  • History and continuing banks
Mobile app — web play not offered
App 03

T&G Holdem

A Texas Hold’em poker simulation for up to six players. Each player receives two private hole cards and forms the strongest five-card hand using the community cards revealed across the flop, turn, and river. Choose No Limit, Pot Limit, or Fixed Limit play, set blinds and antes, and manage bets with chips, Undo, Max Bet, and All In controls.

Local games include human and robot players with selectable action speed. Private multi-phone rooms are human-only and synchronize the dealer button, blinds, board, pot, turn, legal actions, side pots, showdown, and updated banks while limiting private cards to the controlling phone.

  • Up to 6 players
  • No, Pot, or Fixed Limit
  • Selectable blinds and antes
  • Human and robot local play
  • Private multi-phone rooms
  • Hints, history, and music
Mobile app — web play not offered
App 04

T&G 7 Card Stud

A fixed-limit stud poker simulation for two to six players. There are no community cards: each player may receive seven cards—three private and four exposed—and uses the best five-card poker hand at showdown. The game handles antes, the low-card bring-in, small-bet and big-bet streets, configured raise limits, folds, split pots, and standard poker hand rankings.

Local pass-and-play supports human and robot opponents, with Player 1 always human. Private code-based multi-phone rooms keep each seat’s down cards private while synchronizing exposed cards, betting, turn order, and results.

  • 2–6 players
  • Fixed-limit betting
  • Seven cards; best five count
  • Human and robot play
  • Private multi-phone rooms
  • Custom ante and betting levels
Mobile app — web play not offered
App 05

T&G 7 Card Draw

A draw-poker game for two to six human or robot players. Each active player receives seven cards, selects cards to discard or stands pat, and uses the strongest five-card combination at showdown. Chip controls support opening bets, calls, a permitted raise, and folds while respecting the table limits and each player’s available bank.

The Hint and robot systems use statistical hand analysis without seeing hidden hands or future cards. Named human bank history and the last-used settings are saved. Human-only private multi-phone rooms protect each player’s cards while the server controls the deck, legal actions, replacement cards, and showdown.

  • 2–6 players
  • Seven cards; best five count
  • Draw or stand pat
  • Human and robot local play
  • Hints and saved history
  • Private multi-phone rooms
Mobile app — web play not offered
