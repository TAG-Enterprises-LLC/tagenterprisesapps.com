How to Play All Fives

Score when the open ends add up to a multiple of five.

The Goal

Score points by making the open ends add up to a multiple of five. The first player to reach the target score chosen in Settings (150 by default) wins.

Setup

The game uses the double-six set of 28 dominoes. With 2 players each is dealt 7; with 3 or 4 players each is dealt 5. The remaining dominoes form the boneyard. The highest double opens the round.

Playing

  • On your turn, play a domino that matches the number at either open end of the chain.
  • The first double is the spinner. Once dominoes have been played on both sides of it, players may also play on its upper and lower ends, for four open ends in all.
  • If you cannot play, draw from the boneyard until you can play. If the boneyard is empty, you pass.

Scoring

After each play, add the numbers at all open ends; a double at an end counts both halves. If the total is a multiple of five, you score that total. A player who goes out (or has the fewest pips in a blocked game) scores the pips in the other hands, rounded to the nearest five.
