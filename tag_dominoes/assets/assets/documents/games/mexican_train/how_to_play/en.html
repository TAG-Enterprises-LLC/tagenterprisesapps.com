How to Play Mexican Train

Build your train from the engine double.

The Goal

Get rid of all your dominoes in every round. The rounds start from an engine double (12-12 in the first round by default) and count down to 0-0. The player with the lowest total after the last round wins.

Setup

Each round the engine double is placed in the centre. With the double-12 set each player is dealt 15 dominoes (2–4 players) or 12 (5–6 players); smaller sets deal fewer. The rest form the boneyard.

The Trains

Every player has a personal train growing from the engine. There is also the Mexican Train, which anyone may play on. Your own train is always open to you; another player’s train is open only while it carries a marker.

Playing

  • Play one domino per turn, matching the end of a train.
  • If you cannot play, draw one domino and play it if you can. If you still cannot play, pass and put a marker on your own train, opening it to the other players. Playing on your own train removes your marker.
  • After a double, the same player must play again on it. If the double is not satisfied, it stays open and every player must play on it before anything else.

Scoring

When a player plays their last domino, every other player adds the pips left in their hand to their total. If the round is blocked, everyone adds the pips in their hand. The lowest total after the last round wins.
