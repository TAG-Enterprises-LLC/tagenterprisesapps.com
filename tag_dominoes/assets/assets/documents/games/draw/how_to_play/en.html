How to Play Draw

Match the ends and draw when you are stuck.

The Goal

Be the first to play all of your dominoes, or hold the fewest pips when the game is blocked. Rounds are played until someone reaches the target score chosen in Settings.

Setup

The game uses the double-six set of 28 dominoes. With 2 players each is dealt 7; with 3 or 4 players each is dealt 5. The remaining dominoes form the boneyard. The highest double opens the round.

Playing

  • On your turn, play a domino that matches the number at either open end of the chain.
  • If you cannot play, draw from the boneyard until you can play. If the boneyard is empty, you pass.
  • Doubles are placed crosswise; they do not open extra ends.

Winning a Round

The round ends when a player plays their last domino or nobody can play (the game is blocked). The player who goes out scores the pips left in all the other hands. If the game is blocked, the player with the fewest pips scores the pips in the other hands; a tie scores nothing.
