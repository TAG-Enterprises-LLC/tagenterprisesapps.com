How to Play Bergen

Score when the two open ends match.

The Goal

Score points by making the two open ends match. The first player to reach the target score chosen in Settings (15 by default) wins.

Setup

The game uses the double-six set of 28 dominoes. Each player is dealt 6, and the remaining dominoes form the boneyard. The highest double opens the round.

Playing

  • On your turn, play a domino that matches the number at either open end of the chain.
  • If you cannot play, draw from the boneyard until you can play. If the boneyard is empty, you pass.
  • Doubles are placed crosswise; they do not open extra ends.

Scoring

After each play, if both open ends show the same number you score 2 points; if both ends are doubles you score 3 points. The player who goes out (or has the fewest pips in a blocked game) also scores 1 point.
