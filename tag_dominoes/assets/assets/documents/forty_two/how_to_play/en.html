How to Play 42

Texas 42: a trick-taking game played with dominoes.

The Goal

42 is played by four players in two partnerships. Partners sit opposite each other: players 1 and 3 against players 2 and 4. Teams win marks by making their bids. The first team to reach the mark total set in Settings (7 by default) wins the game.

The Dominoes

The game uses a double-six set of 28 dominoes. Each player is dealt 7. Every hand is worth 42 points: 1 point for each of the 7 tricks, plus the count dominoes:

  • 5-5 and 6-4 – 10 points each
  • 5-0, 4-1 and 3-2 – 5 points each

Bidding

Starting with the player to the dealer's left, each player bids once or passes. A bid names the points your team promises to win, from 30 up to 41. A bid of 42 promises to win every trick. A bid of 84 promises every trick and is worth two marks. Each bid must be higher than the last.

If everyone else passes, the dealer must bid 30 (or the hand is redealt if the forced bid setting is off).

Trump

The high bidder chooses the trump: any number from blanks to sixes, all doubles, or (if enabled) no trump.

Playing a Trick

  • The bid winner leads first; after that, the winner of each trick leads.
  • A domino belongs to its higher end (a 6-2 is a six). Doubles belong to their own number. Any domino containing the trump number is trump.
  • The led domino sets the suit. You must follow suit if you can; otherwise you may play anything, including trump.
  • The highest trump wins the trick; if there is no trump, the highest domino of the led suit wins. Doubles are the highest in their suit.

Scoring a Hand

Your team scores the points from every trick it wins. If the bidding team reaches its bid it earns the marks for the bid (one mark, or two for 84). If it falls short it is set, and the other team earns those marks instead.

Playing on One Device

You can mix human and robot players in Setup. When more than one person plays, the game asks you to pass the device before each person's turn so hands stay private.
